# C002 PA-PVDF flux decline analysis in FGDW

## 1. Why this cycle exists

Interpret a stronger flux decline pattern observed for PA-PVDF in FGDW.

## 2. Inputs

- Comparative processed flux data
- Framing guidance from C001

## 3. What was done

Compared processed flux curves and summarized the most defensible interpretation.

## 4. Key observations

PA-PVDF showed a stronger decline than the comparison membrane in the processed dataset.

## 5. Interpretation

The current interpretation is that the modified interface may increase susceptibility to deposition under complex feed conditions.

## 6. Decision

Promote a cautious reusable claim and keep mechanism unresolved.

## 7. Uncertainties

The current dataset cannot distinguish organic fouling, inorganic scaling, and mixed fouling.

## 8. Next steps

Use the current interpretation in AI discussions, but push the model to preserve uncertainty.
