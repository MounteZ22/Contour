# CLM_001 PA-PVDF may be more susceptible to deposition or mixed fouling in FGDW

## Claim

In FGDW, the modified interface may become more susceptible to deposition or mixed fouling, contributing to stronger flux decline than the comparison membrane.

## Supporting evidence

- C002 observed a stronger processed flux decline for PA-PVDF.
- The interpretation was explicitly framed against overclaiming in C001.

## Uncertainty

The current evidence does not isolate a single mechanism and should not be treated as definitive scaling proof.

## Recommended wording

“PA-PVDF may be more susceptible to deposition or mixed fouling in FGDW.”

## Avoid wording

“PA-PVDF is definitively more prone to scaling.”
